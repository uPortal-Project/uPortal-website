# Community

uPortal is built and maintained by a global community of higher-education technologists. Whether you run a portal in production, build portlets, write documentation, or are just exploring the project for the first time, there is room for you here.

The community runs on open mailing lists, public GitHub repositories, and the annual Open Apereo conference. The bar to participate is intentionally low: ask questions, share what you've built, propose changes, and — if you stick around — get recognized as a committer.
