# uPortal Roadmap

The roadmap captures where the uPortal community is heading — not just the uPortal core, but the broader ecosystem of portlets, web components, and supporting projects.

## Why we publish a roadmap

The purpose is to align development with what implementers actually need. Roadmap items emerge from the mailing lists, an annual roadmap discussion at the Open Apereo conference, and ongoing input from active committers. The bar to *propose* an item is low; the bar to *land* one is finding someone willing and able to deliver it.

## How roadmap items are categorized

- **Differentiating** — work that makes uPortal stand out from other portals
- **Integration** — connectivity to back-end services and APIs
- **Parity** — keeping uPortal current and relevant (language versions, build tools, runtime upgrades)
- **Dropped** — items the community has decided not to pursue, even if they have merit

## Current direction

The active multi-year plan is maintained in the [uPortal docs repository](https://github.com/uPortal-Project/uportal-project.github.io/blob/master/roadmap.md) and revisited each year. Recent themes include:

- Modernizing the rendered portal experience and converting portlets to web components
- Revamping the rendering pipeline
- Keeping the runtime stack on supported versions of Java, Maven, and Tomcat
- Retiring the legacy portlet container in favor of web components and Spring controllers

## Ongoing work that doesn't sit on the roadmap

- Security patches (coordinated by the [uPortal Steering Committee](../about/governance.html))
- Documentation maintenance and migration
