# Paid Support

There are two complementary ways to put money behind uPortal: a **Supporting Subscription** to the project itself (funds collective community work), or a **commercial support contract** with a partner (funds your institution's specific deployment).

## uPortal Supporting Subscription

The uPortal Supporting Subscription program is how institutions can financially support the project itself. Subscriptions sit on top of the standard Apereo Member/Affiliate rates and are managed by the [uPortal Steering Committee](../about/governance.html), which determines how the funds are used. Past and ongoing uses include:

- Coordination and engineering of releases
- Testing
- Bug fixing
- Fit and finish of code and documentation pull requests
- Welcoming and bootstrapping new contributors

### Subscription levels

| Tier      | Annual amount | Designation |
| --------- | ------------- | ----------- |
| Bronze    | $1,000        | uPortal Friend |
| Silver    | $5,000        | uPortal Booster |
| Gold      | $10,000       | uPortal Sustainer |
| Platinum  | $20,000       | uPortal Champion |

### What Supporting Subscribers get

- The ability to specify a local Supporting Subscriber contact
- A vote for the Supporting Subscriber representative on the Steering Committee
- **Advance notice of uPortal security patches**
- Recognition as a Supporting Subscriber on this site
- The community's gratitude

To start a subscription, write to the [Apereo Foundation](https://www.apereo.org/membership) or to `uportal-steering-committee@apereo.org`.

## Commercial support

If you need a paid engagement — installation, customization, upgrade work, hosted operations, training, or incident response — the long-standing commercial partner for uPortal is:

- **[Unicon, Inc.](https://www.unicon.net/)** — Apereo Commercial Affiliate that has been involved with uPortal since the early years of the project. Unicon currently employs several of uPortal's active committers and provides commercial support contracts to institutions running uPortal.

The broader [Apereo Commercial Affiliates](https://www.apereo.org/commercial-affiliates) program lists firms that support Apereo projects more generally.

## When to use which

- **Paying to keep the project healthy?** Supporting Subscription.
- **Paying for someone to run or extend uPortal at your institution?** Commercial support partner.
- **Both.** Many institutions do both, and the two models reinforce each other: subscription dollars improve the upstream that commercial partners build on.
