# Support

Support for uPortal comes from three places: the community itself, the project's published documentation, and commercial partners who offer paid support contracts to institutions that need them.

This section is the jump-off point for everything an evaluator, deployer, administrator, or developer might need.

## In this section

- [User Guide](user-guide.html) — a walkthrough of the uPortal demo so you can see what end users experience
- [Administrator Guide](admin-guide.html) — the admin tools that let you manage layouts, portlets, groups, and permissions
- [Deployment Guide](deployment-guide.html) — install uPortal locally to evaluate or to bootstrap a real deployment
- [Developer Guide](developer-guide.html) — how to build, extend, and contribute to uPortal and its supporting projects
- [GitHub Repositories](repositories.html) — annotated list of every active repo in the uPortal ecosystem
- [Paid Support](paid-support.html) — Apereo subscriber program and commercial support partners
