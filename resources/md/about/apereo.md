# Apereo Foundation

The [Apereo Foundation](https://www.apereo.org/) is the non-profit home of uPortal. Founded in 2012 from the merger of the Jasig and Sakai foundations, Apereo's mission is to help higher education organizations sustain, develop, and adopt open-source software that supports learning, teaching, and research.

## What Apereo provides to uPortal

- **Legal and licensing structure** — uPortal is released under the Apache License 2.0; Apereo holds the project's trademarks and contributor agreements
- **Community infrastructure** — mailing lists at `groups.google.com/a/apereo.org`, the `uportal-steering-committee@apereo.org` address, the annual Open Apereo conference where roadmap discussions happen, and the welcoming policy that informs our [Code of Conduct](../community/code-of-conduct.html)
- **Governance scaffolding** — the model that the [uPortal Steering Committee](governance.html) operates within, including how Supporting Subscriber representatives are elected

## Sister projects

Other Apereo-stewarded projects you may recognize:

- **Sakai** — open-source learning management system
- **CAS** — Central Authentication Service, used by many uPortal deployments for SSO
- **Bedework** — calendar and scheduling system
- **Karuta** — ePortfolio platform
- **OpenEquella** — open-source content repository

A full list lives at [apereo.org/projects](https://www.apereo.org/projects).

## Membership and subscription

Apereo is funded by member institutions and project-level Supporting Subscriptions. uPortal participates in the latter model — see [Paid Support](../support/paid-support.html) for how the uPortal Supporting Subscription program funds project work.
