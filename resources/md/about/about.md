# About

uPortal is an open-source portal framework built by and for the higher education community. Originally released in 2000, it remains one of the longest-running open-source projects in higher ed and continues to power campus portals at universities, K-12 districts, and research organizations worldwide.

The project is stewarded by the uPortal Steering Committee (uPSC) under the umbrella of the [Apereo Foundation](apereo.html), the same non-profit that supports projects like Sakai, CAS, and OpenEquella. Day-to-day development is community-driven: anyone can contribute, and active contributors can be elected as committers.
