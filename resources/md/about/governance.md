# uPortal Governance

The **uPortal Steering Committee (uPSC)** is the governing body responsible for strategic, financial, and operational oversight of the uPortal project. The committee advocates for the project, fosters communication within the community, coordinates security response, and helps identify the people and funding needed to keep uPortal healthy.

You can reach the committee at `uportal-steering-committee@apereo.org`.

## Responsibilities

The uPSC:

- Coordinates community calls, online hangouts, and conference meetings
- Oversees project documentation and marketing materials
- Coordinates response to uPortal security issues
- Helps identify and develop resources (people and funding) for the project
- Liaises between uPortal developers, deployers, and the Apereo Board

## Composition

uPSC members serve two-year terms and can be re-elected or re-appointed. The committee is structured to bring different perspectives to the table:

- **Developer representatives** (2–4) — nominated and elected by active committers
- **Community representatives** (2–4) — nominated by anyone in the community, elected by the uPSC
- **Supporting Subscriber representative** (1) — nominated and elected by Apereo members that are also uPortal Supporting Subscribers in good standing
- **Apereo Board liaison** (1) — non-voting, appointed by the Apereo Board

Terms are staggered so the committee has continuity year to year.

## How to get involved

If you're interested in how uPortal is run — or you'd like to nominate someone (including yourself) for a future term — start on the [Community](../community/community.html) page or write to the committee directly. The committee's working documents live in the [uPortal-steering-cmte](https://github.com/uPortal-Project/uPortal-steering-cmte) repository on GitHub.
