# Showcase Gallery

A live picture of uPortal in production is the most persuasive thing this site can show. This page is the scaffold for that gallery — the entries below will be filled in as deployers contribute screenshots and short writeups of their portals.

## Featured deployments

> This section is a work in progress. We're inviting deployers to share screenshots and a short description of their uPortal deployment. If you run a uPortal portal and would like it featured here, please reach out on [`uportal-user`](../community/mailing-lists.html) or open an issue on [uPortal-website](https://github.com/uPortal-Project/uPortal-website).

## Active deployers

uPortal currently runs in production at higher-education institutions in North America and Europe. Active deployers known to the community include:

- Cal Poly Pomona
- Cal Poly San Luis Obispo
- ESUP-Portail consortium (France)
- Savannah College of Art and Design
- Sinclair Community College
- State Center Community College District
- Toronto Metropolitan University
- UC Irvine
- UC Riverside
- University of Edinburgh

If your institution runs uPortal and isn't listed here — or if your status has changed — please get in touch so we can keep this current.

## Submit your deployment

Have a uPortal deployment you'd like to highlight?

1. Open an issue on [uPortal-website](https://github.com/uPortal-Project/uPortal-website) titled "Gallery: {Institution Name}"
2. Include: institution, country, a screenshot or two of public-facing pages (or anonymized internal pages), and 2–3 sentences about what makes your deployment notable
3. Confirm you have permission to publish the screenshots

The community will help finalize the entry.
