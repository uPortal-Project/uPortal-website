# Showcase

uPortal powers production portals at hundreds of institutions worldwide. Some are quietly internal; others are public-facing front doors for students, faculty, and staff. Each deployment looks different because uPortal is designed to be heavily personalized — by layout, by theme, and by which portlets and web components are wired in.

This section gives you a sense of what uPortal can do and what deployers are doing with it today.
